# Meeting Preparation Skill — 会议准备

## Goal
From meeting context (company name, attendees, meeting type), produce a structured pre-read: company snapshot → industry trends → agenda strategy → suggested questions → downloadable briefing file.

## Available Tools
search_web, scrape (firecrawl), RunPython, Write, docwriter skill.

## Workflow

### Step 1: Company Snapshot
Use search_web to gather:
- Company basics (founded, size, HQ, funding)
- Recent news (last 6 months)
- Key products/services
- Known pain points or public challenges

### Step 2: Industry Context
Research the broader industry:
- Market trends (growth, consolidation, regulation)
- Key competitors and their recent moves
- Relevant news that the attendees would care about

### Step 3: Attendee Profiles
If attendee names are provided, search for:
- Role and tenure
- Recent public statements, interviews, or posts
- Common interests or professional background

### Step 4: Agenda Strategy
Build a suggested agenda:
- Opening (2 min): connect on shared context
- Discovery (5 min): key questions to uncover needs
- Value proposition (3 min): tailored to their situation
- Next steps (2 min): clear call to action

### Step 5: Executive Briefing
Compile into a structured briefing document:
- 1-page executive summary
- Company profile
- Industry landscape
- 5 suggested questions with rationale
- Risk areas and mitigation talking points

## Quality Rules
- Verify all company facts with at least 2 sources
- Questions should be open-ended and discovery-oriented
- Avoid generic questions — tailor to company specifics
- Mark time allocations as suggestions, not rigid requirements
