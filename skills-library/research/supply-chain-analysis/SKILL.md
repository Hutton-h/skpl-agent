# Supply Chain Analysis Skill — 供应链分析

## Goal
From company or industry context → map supplier networks → identify risks → recommend optimization strategies.

## Available Tools
search_web, scrape, RunPython, docwriter skill.

## Workflow

### Step 1: Network Mapping
Research and map:
- Key suppliers and their locations
- Raw material sources
- Manufacturing and distribution centers
- Logistics partners

### Step 2: Risk Assessment
Analyze risks across:
- Geopolitical: country risk, trade restrictions
- Operational: single-source dependencies, capacity constraints
- Financial: supplier financial health
- Environmental: climate risks, regulatory changes

### Step 3: Optimization Recommendations
- Diversification strategies
- Near-shoring / re-shoring opportunities
- Inventory optimization
- Technology adoption (blockchain, IoT, AI)

### Step 4: Report Delivery
Produce a structured supply chain report with:
- Network diagram / map
- Risk heat map
- Optimization recommendations
- Implementation roadmap

## Quality Rules
- Verify supplier information with at least 2 sources
- Distinguish between confirmed and estimated data
- Consider both short-term disruptions and long-term trends
